STR Revenue Audit compares any short-term rental listing against its local comp pool from free, public Inside Airbnb data — then prints a clean one-page PDF showing the estimated gap and the top three fixes. No scraping, no paid services, no fake precision.

Real output. Sample audit for a live Austin listing: last review in 2020, $0 modelled annual revenue vs a $16,959 comp median — with the formulas printed under every number, and ESTIMATE stamped across the page. Yours could look just like this, minus the awkward conversation with the owner.

How it works

1

Pick a listing

Look one up from the Inside Airbnb Austin snapshot (2026-09-17), enter it manually (unlocks the photo-count signal), or upload a one-row CSV/JSON.

2

Comps get built

Same room type, beds ±1, same neighbourhood (3 km radius fallback), then k-nearest neighbours on scaled price/beds/baths — the nearest 12 listings win.

3

Get the one-pager

Gap estimates, signals (reviews, description, photos, price vs comps), ranked fixes, and an LLM summary in plain English — as a print-ready PDF.

Honest by construction

ESTIMATES, not invoices

Two estimates only: A = (comp median − your price) × comp occupancy × 365 and B = comp median revenue − yours (Inside Airbnb model, active comps only). Both formulas are printed on the page. No invented per-signal dollars.

Free, public data only

Inside Airbnb snapshot for Austin, TX (2026-09-17, CC BY 4.0). No Airbnb scraping — the loader is ~100 lines and the CSV is the only input.

Fixes you can action Monday

Signals are plain English: reviews dried up, description under 60 words, price 10%+ off the comp median, photos under the benchmark — ranked by severity, not vibes.

Reproducible pipeline

pip install -r requirements.txt → python scripts/download_data.py → python -m str_audit.audit --listing-id … → python -m str_audit.report --listing-id …

The tool

Upload or enter a listing, watch the audit assemble, download the PDF. Photo counts are the one thing public data can't see, so they happen here — in the browser, never stored.

Want this for your portfolio?

The whole thing is open source: pipeline, comps engine, PDF renderer, Streamlit shell, tests. Inside Airbnb attribution required (CC BY 4.0). No license file yet — ask before reuse.
