What an audit looks like →

The artifact your leads receive

One page. Their listing vs its local comp pool. A conservative range of estimated annual upside (never a single invented number), the three fixes ranked by impact, and every formula printed at the bottom. No jargon — a non-technical owner can act on it.

ESTIMATE, honestly labeled

Two public-data estimates only — price positioning vs the comp median, and the Inside Airbnb revenue model vs active comps. Formula printed right on the page. If the comp set is too thin, the report says INSUFFICIENT DATA instead of quoting a figure.

Fixes a human can ship Monday

Rewrite the description, re-shoot the hero photos, re-price — each fix ranked by practical severity, each tied to what it costs the listing in bookings or rate. No consulting theatre.

No invented revenue numbers

The audit is a model, not an invoice. It says so on the page. The range is conservative — the low end is what repricing alone recovers; the high end is comp-median performance. Nobody gets told a promise.

How it's built

For the technically curious. The pipeline is a deterministic, auditable sequence — nothing about it requires trusting an LLM with your numbers. LLMs only write the last summary line (and a deterministic offline summary exists when no API key is set).

Data
Inside Airbnb, "Austin, Texas" snapshot 2026-09-17 (CC BY 4.0). 11,005 listings, public fields only; no scraping, no ToS gray zone.
Comp pool
Same room type, beds ±1, same neighbourhood (radius > metro fallback), k-nearest on scaled price/beds/baths — 12 comps per subject.
Est. gap
A: (comp median − your price) × comp occupancy × 365 · B: median(active comp estimated_revenue_l365d) − subject. Headline = the conservative A→B range. Below 8 dissimilar comps → INSUFFICIENT DATA and no figure is printed at all.
Health
100 − 25 per failing signal − 12 per warning − 5 for unknown photo count (public data can't count photos). Formula printed on the page.
Stack
Python · pandas/DuckDB · scikit-learn · Streamlit · Jinja2 + xhtml2pdf PDF · Gemini/Groq free tiers (offline fallback when absent).
